      KNOW WHAT TO LOOK FOR

      Red flags worth
      paying attention to.

      Too good to be true

      Unusually high pay for very little work or no experience.

      Pay to get paid

      Requests for registration fees, deposits, or training payments.

      Artificial urgency

      Pressure to act immediately or lose an exclusive opportunity.

      Personal data requests

      Early requests for OTPs, passwords, banking details, or identity documents.

      GOOD QUESTIONS TO ASK

      Frequently asked questions.

      Can ScamShield guarantee that a message is safe?

      No. This demo checks a small set of common warning signs. A message with no detected red flags may still be fraudulent.

      Does the scanner use a real AI model?

      Not yet. This version uses simple local keyword rules for demonstration and educational purposes. A real AI service would require a properly configured backend.

      Should I pay a fee to get a job?

      Be very cautious. Requests for upfront payments to secure employment are a significant warning sign. Independently verify the company and offer before proceeding.

      What should I do if I suspect a scam in India?

      Do not send money or share more information. Preserve evidence and report suspected cybercrime through official government channels, such as cybercrime.gov.in. If money has been lost, contact your bank promptly and consider calling India's cybercrime helpline at 1930.
